# 📖 Bookmark Feature Guide

## What is the Bookmark Button?

The **Bookmark button** (📑 icon) in the PDF viewer allows you to save specific pages in your PDFs so you can easily return to them later.

## How to Use Bookmarks

### Adding a Bookmark
1. Open any PDF file
2. Navigate to the page you want to bookmark
3. Click the **Bookmark button** (📑 icon) in the PDF controls
4. You'll see a green checkmark ✓ and a notification: "Bookmark added: Page X"

### What Gets Saved?
When you bookmark a page, the following information is saved:
- **PDF Name** - The name of the book/document
- **Page Number** - The exact page you bookmarked
- **Date & Time** - When you created the bookmark
- **File Path** - Location of the PDF file

### Viewing Your Bookmarks
Your bookmarks are saved in the **Bookmarks Panel**. To view them:
1. Look for the bookmarks list in the sidebar
2. Click on any bookmark to jump directly to that page in the PDF

### Keyboard Shortcut
- Press **B** key while viewing a PDF to quickly add a bookmark

## Benefits of Bookmarks

✅ **Quick Access** - Jump directly to important pages  
✅ **Multiple Bookmarks** - Save as many pages as you need  
✅ **Persistent** - Bookmarks are saved even after closing the browser  
✅ **Organized** - See all your bookmarks with page numbers and dates  
✅ **Easy Navigation** - Perfect for long religious texts and scriptures  

## Example Use Cases

- Mark your favorite prayers or mantras
- Save pages you want to read daily
- Bookmark important sections for quick reference
- Mark pages for study or memorization
- Keep track of multiple readings across different books

## Technical Details

- Bookmarks are stored in your browser's **localStorage**
- They persist across browser sessions
- Each bookmark includes the PDF path, page number, and timestamp
- No limit on the number of bookmarks you can create

---

**Note:** Bookmarks are stored locally in your browser. If you clear your browser data or use a different device, you'll need to create bookmarks again.
